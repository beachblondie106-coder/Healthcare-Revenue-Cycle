# KPI Definitions

| KPI | Definition |
|---|---|
| Total claims | Distinct claim count in the active filter context |
| Submitted charges | Sum of `claim_charge` |
| Expected allowed amount | Sum of contractually expected allowed amounts |
| Actual payer payments | Sum of payer payments received by the reporting date |
| Outstanding balance | Remaining collectible claim balance at the reporting date |
| Initial denial rate | Initially denied adjudicated insured claims divided by adjudicated insured claims |
| Final denial rate | Claims remaining denied after appeal/resubmission divided by adjudicated insured claims |
| Underpayment rate | Paid claims flagged as underpaid divided by paid claims |
| Underpayment amount | Positive difference between expected and actual payer reimbursement for underpaid claims |
| Payment realization rate | Actual payer payment divided by expected payer payment for the applicable reporting population |
| Appeal success rate | Successful completed appeals divided by completed appeals |
| Denial recovery rate | Recovered denied dollars divided by denied dollars pursued |
| Aging bucket | Outstanding claims grouped into 0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 91–120, and over-120-day bands |
| Follow-up priority | Rule-based band combining balance, age, denial status, underpayment, and filing risk |

Dashboard totals may differ from broad validation totals when a visual applies a narrower denominator, such as paid, adjudicated, or insured claims. Each comparison should be interpreted within its active filters.
